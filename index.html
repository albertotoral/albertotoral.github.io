AT Publisher is a private tool used by the Spanish artist Alberto Toral (flamenco / pop singer, label Fiesta Music) to publish his own original music videos on his own social media accounts.

What it does

  • The artist prepares his own videos (lyric videos, live performance clips and animated videos of his mascot) on his computer.
  • He chooses the account, the caption and the date and time.
  • AT Publisher uploads the video to his own TikTok account as a draft (TikTok inbox), where he reviews and publishes it himself in the TikTok app, and to his own YouTube channel as a scheduled YouTube Short.

Who can use it

Only the artist himself, with his own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
