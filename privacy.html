AT Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by Alberto Toral (Granada, Spain; contact: alberto.toral.re@gmail.com) to upload his own videos to his own TikTok account and YouTube channel.

1. Data we access

  • TikTok: with the artist's permission (TikTok Login Kit), the app reads basic profile information (display name and avatar, scope user.info.basic) to show which account is connected, and uploads videos to his TikTok inbox as drafts (scope video.upload).
  • YouTube: with the artist's permission (Google OAuth), the app uploads videos to his own channel and sets their title, description and publish time (scope youtube.upload), and reads the channel name to show which channel is connected.

2. How we use it

Only to upload the artist's own videos to his own accounts when he asks. The app does not read, collect or store data about other users, viewers, followers or comments.

3. Storage

Access tokens and a simple upload log (file name, platform, date and the returned video ID) are stored only on the artist's own computer. Nothing is stored on external servers by the app. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

4. YouTube API Services

AT Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the user agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

5. TikTok

Use of TikTok features is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).

6. Deleting data

To delete all data, revoke access as above and delete the app folder from the computer. You can also write to alberto.toral.re@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Contact

alberto.toral.re@gmail.com
